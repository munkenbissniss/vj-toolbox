VJ Toolbox

Tools for people who make VJ loops and digital visuals.

Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your computer.

1. Find loops

Sources:
Free API keys (Pexels, Pixabay)

Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons work without an account. Pexels and Pixabay have a lot of ready-made loops and are free, but need a key (takes a minute): Pexels · Pixabay. Keys are saved in this browser only and are sent only to Pexels / Pixabay.

Licences differ per clip (Pexels and Pixabay are free to use; Commons and Archive clips may need credit). The author and licence of every download are saved in credits.csv, which comes with your downloaded files.

Or paste links

Only download things you have the right to use. Some sites do not allow a web page to download their files; then use “Open page”, download the clip yourself and drop it into the Convert tab.
